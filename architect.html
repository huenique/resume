Selected Decisions

  • Build vs. buy. Website traffic data usually means paying a panel provider. I built an estimator that costs $0.93 per site and ties SimilarWeb on US commercial sites, then documented where it loses, on non-US sites, so the team knows when to trust it and when to buy.
  • Hosted vs. self-hosted. A video analysis platform could run on a self-hosted vision model or a hosted one. I benchmarked both on the real workload, chose the hosted model at 3–5× lower cost and 2× the speed, and designed the 14-agent orchestrator so it could be reused. A second product was built on it in two weeks.
  • Rejecting a newer model. For live speaker identification, I tested a streaming diarization model and rejected it at 19.8% error against the existing pipeline’s 3.5%, then cut that pipeline’s alert delay from 1.16 to 0.18 seconds.
  • Stopping a launch. An options yield product looked like steady income. A 20,000-path Monte Carlo and a five-year backtest showed no edge beyond the volatility risk premium, and a small assumption error flipped it negative, so I stopped the capital deployment.
