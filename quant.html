Selected Results

  • A win rate that lost money. A Polymarket fleet won 96.6% of 119 trades and still had negative expected value, because one loss cost as much as 57 wins and break-even needed 98.2%.
  • An edge that vanished with size. A maker-taker strategy tested positive at $1 per leg because earlier tests assumed any crossed level filled in full. I built a fill model that walks the trade tape’s real sizes, and the edge turned negative between $1 and $10 per leg.
  • An unwind that never filled. IOC exit orders priced at the last-seen bid missed every time in a falling market while buys at the ask kept filling. I moved exits to price through the book and logged every zero-fill, since the venue keeps no record of them.
  • A kill switch that could not fire. The grid computed P&L in contracts, so on a 1,000-unit contract a $5 loss read as 0.005 and the switch, set in quote currency, never triggered. P&L is now in quote currency everywhere, including the kill switch.
  • A scan too slow to act on. Opportunities disappeared before the scanner finished a pass. Replacing per-symbol requests with three bulk calls cut a scan of 528 markets from 79 seconds to 1.3.
